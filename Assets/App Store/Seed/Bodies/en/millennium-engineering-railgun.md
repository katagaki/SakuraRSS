The Engineering Department showed off its newest invention on Thursday afternoon: a railgun light enough for one student to carry, and quiet enough, the club says, to fire "without setting off every alarm in the building".

Club president Utaha said the design started as a weekend project. "We wanted to know how small we could make it before it stopped working," she said. "It turns out the answer is: smaller than we expected."

The new railgun weighs about a third as much as the Super Nova, the department's best-known creation, and charges in under a minute from an ordinary wall socket. The team replaced the old capacitor bank with a set of stacked cells that fold into the stock, which is where most of the weight savings came from.

## A name before a launch

Before the department had finished its presentation, Aris had already asked to hold it, and had already given it a name. "It is called the Light of Dawn," she told the audience. "Aris has decided."

The department says it will keep the name.

## Seminar asks for a review

Not everyone was as excited. Seminar has asked for a full safety review before the railgun is fired anywhere indoors, and treasurer Yuuka has asked for a full review of the department's receipts.

"I am happy for them," Yuuka said. "I would be happier if they had told us about the stacked cells before they bought forty of them."

The Engineering Department expects the review to take two weeks. Until then, the railgun will be on display, unloaded, in the lobby of Millennium Tower.
