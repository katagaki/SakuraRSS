By the Kronos School of Journalism

For one afternoon, the busiest office in Kivotos stopped taking requests and started taking visitors.

Schale held its first open house on Saturday, and by noon the line outside the Federal Investigation Club's office stretched past the station. Students from Millennium, Trinity, Gehenna and Abydos waited side by side, some for more than an hour, for a chance to see where Sensei works.

![](image:schale-open-house-line)

## Inside the office

Sensei led every tour personally. Visitors saw the desk where requests are sorted, the shelf of borrowed umbrellas that have never been returned, and the tablet where Arona keeps the schedule.

"I wanted everyone to know they can come here," Sensei said between tours. "Not only when something is on fire. Although, also when something is on fire."

Arona answered questions from the tablet all afternoon, and handed out a small card to every visitor with the new way to send a request through MomoTalk.

## Snacks, and a short queue for photos

The Gourmet Research Society ran a snack table at the entrance, and, for once, nothing was blown up. The Game Development Department set up a corner where visitors could play an early build of Tales Saga Chronicle 2.

By the end of the day, Schale had welcomed more than six hundred students. Asked whether there would be a second open house, Sensei laughed. "Ask me again after I have caught up on the paperwork."
